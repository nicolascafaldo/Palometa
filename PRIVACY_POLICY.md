## 1. Privacy Policy (Zero Data Collection)

Palometa operates **100% locally on your device**. The developer does not operate servers for this app and does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal or device data.

- **No Personal Information Collected:** No account creation, login, email, name, device identifiers, location, or usage telemetry is collected.
- **No Analytics or Advertising SDKs:** Palometa contains zero ads, zero tracking SDKs, and zero third-party analytics.
- **Local File & App Access:**
  - When you open an `.apk` file or mount a folder using Android's Storage Access Framework (`ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT` / `ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT_TREE`), Palometa reads only the files you explicitly choose.
  - When you browse **Installed Apps**, Palometa uses Android's targeted launcher `<queries>` filter (`ACTION_MAIN` / `CATEGORY_LAUNCHER`) to list launchable apps locally on your device. This list and any parsed APK contents remain strictly on your device.
  - Temporary cached copies used for parsing (`cache/workspace/`) and bundle generation (`cache/exports/`) remain inside the app's private cache directory.
- **User-Initiated Exports:** ZIP bundles generated in Export Studio (`*_ghidra_bundle.zip`) are written or shared via Android's share sheet only when you explicitly press **Save Bundle (.zip)** or **Share to Workstation**.
- **Children's Privacy:** Palometa is a developer and security auditing utility intended for adults (18+) and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
